# How Chamba scales

Workers never pay. Chamba connects them, and the companies that hire them pay.

## Stage 1: Grow the pool with UGC (user-generated content)

- **Workers bring workers.** Talent cards, invite links with a rich preview, and short videos workers post about getting accepted.
- **No payouts to workers.** We don't cash out bonuses or run a wallet. What a worker gets is a verified talent card and a faster path to a real job.
- **Proof first.** Every accepted application with a verified screenshot is a placement we can show.

## Stage 2: Act as the middleman through referral programs

- **Our referral code on every "Postular" link.** AI-training platforms (Mercor, Outlier, Alignerr and others) pay us when someone we sent gets hired or starts working.
- **Placements tracked end to end:** applied → accepted → verified proof. The acceptance rate per platform is the number we sell.
- **Partner programs over personal codes.** Where a platform's terms ban posting personal referral links publicly or recruiting as an agency, we use its partner or recruiting program instead. Each platform's terms are checked before we turn its link on.
- **First targets (terms checked 2026-11-15):**
  - **SuperAnnotate's SME Careers:** it has a partner type for recruiting agencies and businesses, and its links keep the code on a deep link to a specific job.
  - **Mercor:** public referral links are allowed. It pays 20% of the referred worker's earnings, up to 4× their first hourly rate, and covers MX, CO, AR, PE and CL. Sign-ups are capped at 100 per rolling 30 days.
  - **Excluded:** Braintrust, Prolific and Handshake AI, whose terms ban public or app-based referral links.
- **Known limits:**
  - Referral programs mostly pay a person, not a company.
  - A platform can change or end its program at any time.
  - A worker who already has an account there earns us nothing.
  
  These limits are why stage 3 matters.

## Stage 3: Sell the pool to companies directly

- **A large, pre-screened, verified Spanish-speaking pool is the asset.** Once it's big enough, we go to AI labs and data vendors directly, without a platform in between.
- **Revenue:** placement fees or a margin on hours worked, plus paid access to the filtered talent pool (/talento with country, dialect, skill, score and verified history).
- **Why companies pay:** they get people who already passed a pre-screen and an AI interview, which saves them screening cost.

## Stage 4: New countries and languages

- **The model doesn't change: onboarding plus the pre-screen.** Adding a market means:
  - translating the UI and adding the language's variants to onboarding,
  - writing pre-screen questions and an interview rubric in that language,
  - importing that market's postings.
- **Order:** Portuguese (Brazil) first, then other high-demand languages where AI labs are short of native speakers.
- **The scoring, affinity and anti-gaming logic stays shared.** Only content and data are per market.

## What stays fixed at every stage

- Chamba is free for workers and never asks them for money or bank details.
- Chamba is not the employer.
- Only verified placements count in any number we show companies.
